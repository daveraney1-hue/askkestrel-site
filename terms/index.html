The free check

  • It's free, with no obligation to buy anything.

What a report is, and isn't

  • A report shows what ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity and Claude said when we asked, on the date shown. AI answers vary from one ask to the next and change over time, so a later check can give different results.
  • Reports are based on AI answers and public information, such as Google listings and websites. They can contain mistakes, and the AI apps themselves are sometimes wrong.
  • The Kestrel Score measures how often AI apps named your business in our checks. It isn't a rating of your business and it isn't a promise of future results.
  • Reports are general information to help you decide what to do. They aren't legal, financial or other professional advice, and you decide which changes to make.
  • Anyone with the private link to a report can open it, so share the link carefully.

No guarantees

The companies that run AI apps decide what their apps say, and they change how the apps work without notice. Nobody can guarantee a business a place in AI answers, and we don't. We don't promise any particular mention, score, ranking, traffic, leads or sales.

What we do promise is to do the work we describe, check the same way each time, and report the results honestly.

Kestrel plans

  • Setup fee (Fix & Track): Fix & Track has a one-time setup fee, which we quote after your consultation and bill separately by invoice. It's fully refundable until we start the setup work. Once the work has started, it isn't refundable.
  • Payment: plans are billed monthly in advance, in Canadian dollars, plus applicable taxes. Payments are processed securely by Stripe; we never see or store your full card number.
  • Your card and receipts: use Billing in your Kestrel portal to update your card, change your billing details and see or download your receipts.

If it's still unpaid 14 days after it was due, we pause your reports and work until it's paid. If it's still unpaid 30 days after it was due, your plan ends and any spot you hold in an area is released.
  • Discounts and promo codes: a discount or promo code applies only as described when we give it, for the time stated, and only to the plan or items it names. Discounts can't be exchanged for cash or carried over, and unless we say otherwise there's one per business. When a discount ends, your regular price applies.
  • Spots per area: on Fix & Track and Grow we take on at most three businesses of the same type in each area, counted across both plans. Your area is the town or city your tracked questions ask about, and you can add extra areas on Fix & Track or Grow for an added monthly price. On Fix & Track each extra area gets its own three questions; on Grow an extra area counts once at least two of your questions ask about it. A province-wide or national plan, quoted separately, holds a spot in every area it covers; areas that are already full when it starts are left out. Spots go to clients in the order they sign up and pay, and a spot is released when a plan ends or moves to Track. If an area is full for your type of business, you can join its waitlist, and we offer a spot that opens to the people waiting in the order they joined. Track has no limit. Area sizes and prices are shown on our pricing page.
  • Extra questions: on any plan you can add questions beyond those your plan includes, for $10 a month each, starting from the month we add them. Track goes up to six questions in all. Extra questions are asked about your home area or an area you already pay for; they don't hold a spot in, or count toward, another area. You can drop an extra question at any time by emailing us, and it comes off your next monthly charge.
  • Monthly reports: your first report runs once you confirm your tracked questions in your portal, or 24 hours after we suggest them if you haven't confirmed by then. After that you get a report each month, around the day of the month your plan started. We review every report before it's sent to you.

You stay the owner of those accounts and can remove our access at any time. If we can't get the access we need, we may not be able to finish that work.

  • Ending a plan: we may pause or end a plan if a payment isn't made, as described above, or if these terms are broken. If we end a plan for any other reason, we'll refund any month you've paid for and not used.
  • When a plan ends: your portal stays open until the end of the month you've paid for. 30 days after your plan ends, we delete your portal logins, reports and to-do lists, so download anything you want to keep before then. If you come back later, you start fresh. Stripe keeps billing records for as long as the law requires.
