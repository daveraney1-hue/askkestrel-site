The free check

  • It's free, with no obligation to buy anything.

What a report is, and isn't

  • A report shows what ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity and Claude said when we asked, on the date shown. AI answers vary from one ask to the next and change over time, so a later check can give different results.
  • Reports are based on AI answers and public information, such as Google listings and websites. They can contain mistakes, and the AI apps themselves are sometimes wrong.
  • The Kestrel Score measures how often AI apps named your business in our checks. It isn't a rating of your business and it isn't a promise of future results.
  • Reports are general information to help you decide what to do. They aren't legal, financial or other professional advice, and you decide which changes to make.
  • Anyone with the private link to a report can open it, so share the link carefully.

No guarantees

The companies that run AI apps decide what their apps say, and they change how the apps work without notice. Nobody can guarantee a business a place in AI answers, and we don't. We don't promise any particular mention, score, ranking, traffic, leads or sales.

What we do promise is to do the work we describe, check the same way each time, and report the results honestly.

Kestrel plans

  • What's included and the price are what we confirm with you when you sign up. If we change the price of your plan, we'll tell you by email at least 30 days before it takes effect.
  • Payment: plans are billed monthly in advance, in Canadian dollars, plus applicable taxes.
  • Cancelling: you can cancel at any time by emailing info@askkestrel.ca. Your plan stays active until the end of the month you've paid for, and we don't refund part months unless the law requires it.
  • Spots per area: on Fix & Track and Grow we take on at most three businesses of the same type in each area, counted across both plans. Your area is the town or city your tracked questions ask about, and you can add extra areas on Fix & Track or Grow for an added monthly price. On Fix & Track each extra area gets its own three questions; on Grow an extra area counts once at least two of your questions ask about it. A province-wide or national plan, quoted separately, holds a spot in every area it covers; areas that are already full when it starts are left out. Spots go to clients in the order they sign up and pay, and a spot is released when a plan ends or moves to Track. Track has no limit. Area sizes and prices are shown on our pricing page.
  • What we need from you: on plans where we do the work (Fix & Track and Grow), we'll need access to things like your Google Business Profile, website or directory listings. You stay the owner of those accounts and can remove our access at any time. If we can't get the access we need, we may not be able to finish that work.
  • Your approval: we'll tell you what we plan to change before we change public information about your business, such as your listings or website text.
  • Ending a plan: we may pause or end a plan if a payment isn't made or these terms are broken. If we end a plan for any other reason, we'll refund any month you've paid for and not used.
