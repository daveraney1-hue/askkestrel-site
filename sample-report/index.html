Listings

Get listed on the two directory sites the AI cites most for agents in Maplewood

Kestrel

These two sites shaped 23 of the 36 answers. Northgate and Brookside are on both. You are on neither.

  1. Create a profile on the first site with your team name, phone and website exactly as on Google
  2. Add your service area, photos and a short bio for each agent
  3. Repeat on the second site, then ask five recent clients to review you there

Website

Add a "Selling your home in Maplewood" page

Kestrel

The selling question is where you are weakest: 1 of 12 answers. No page on your site answers it.

  1. Write a page covering your selling process, timeline and how you price a home
  2. Name the neighbourhoods you sell in and link to recent sales
  3. Add it to your menu and link it from your Google profile

Google Profile

Add your service areas and a second category

Kestrel

Your profile lists one category and no service areas. The agents named most list both.

  1. Add "Real estate consultant" as a second category
  2. Add Maplewood and the three nearby towns you serve as service areas
  3. Rewrite the description to mention buying and selling in Maplewood

Ask recent buyers to mention the neighbourhood in reviews

You

AI answers quoted reviews that named a street or neighbourhood. Few of yours do.

  1. Send your review link within a week of each closing

  3. Reply to each review in a sentence that mentions the area
