Questions people ask first

Isn't this just SEO?

It overlaps. A good Google profile, real reviews and a clear website help with both. The difference is what gets measured. SEO tracks where you rank on a page of results. Kestrel tracks whether the AI names you when it gives one answer, and which websites it trusted to decide.

Can't I just ask ChatGPT myself?

You can, and it's worth doing. But the AI apps remember what you've told them and tailor answers to you, so what you see isn't what a stranger sees. One ask also isn't reliable, because the answer changes. We ask each question several times in clean sessions across four apps and keep the history, so you see a trend instead of a guess.

Who is this for?

Any business whose customers pick from a short list: real estate agents, trades, clinics, cafes, shops and professional services. It works whether your customers are in one town, a region or across Canada. Choose which on the form.

Is the check really free?

Yes, once per business. You get the report, and we'll walk you through it if you'd like. There's no obligation. If you want to keep tracking how the AI apps talk about you, that's what the monthly plans are for.

How long until something changes?

Some fixes show up within weeks, especially on apps that search the web live, like Perplexity. Others take a few months to work through. That's why we track monthly rather than judging it on one week.
