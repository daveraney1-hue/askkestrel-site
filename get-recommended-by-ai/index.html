Some digital marketing and SEO agencies also offer this, often under the name "generative engine optimization" (GEO) or "AI search optimization". Whoever you choose, look for three things:

  • They measure before they fix. You should see how often the AI apps name you today, on the questions your customers actually ask.

Frequently asked questions

What is generative engine optimization (GEO)?

GEO is the work of making a business easier for AI assistants like ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity and Claude to find, understand and recommend. It overlaps with SEO, but the goal is different: being named in the AI's answer, not ranking on a page of links.

How do AI assistants decide which businesses to recommend?

They search the web, read a handful of pages such as review sites, directories, articles and business websites, and name the businesses that come up most consistently with good reviews and clear details. What other sites say about you counts heavily. More on how AI search works.

Can anyone guarantee ChatGPT will recommend my business?

No. The AI apps decide for themselves, and answers change from one ask to the next. Be wary of anyone who promises a spot. What a service can do is fix what the AI relies on and measure honestly whether it's working.

How long does it take to start getting recommended?

Some changes show up within weeks, especially on apps that search the web live, like Perplexity. Others take a few months. That's why Kestrel tracks monthly rather than judging results on one week.

Do I need a Google Business Profile?

If you serve customers in a particular area, yes. Gemini searches with Google, and the other apps often read Google results too. A complete, accurate profile with real reviews is one of the first things Kestrel checks.

Does Kestrel only work with businesses in Ontario?

No. Kestrel is based in Orangeville, Ontario, and works with small businesses across Canada. Checks can cover one town, a region or province, or the whole country.
