Extra areas, on Fix & Track or Grow. If you want us to track and work on more than one area, each extra area is added on top of your plan price and takes one of the three spots there.       Questions about pricing

      Why only three businesses of each type per area?

      On Fix & Track and Grow we actively work to get you named. If we did that for twenty agents in the same town, we’d be working against our own clients. An AI answer usually names three to five businesses, so three spots leaves room for every client we take on.

      Why is Track not limited?

      Track is measurement. We check what the AI apps say and tell you what to change, but we don’t do the work, so any number of businesses can use it without getting in each other’s way.

      Why do big cities cost more?

      There’s more competition, so it takes more fixes and more months of adjustments to move the numbers, and a spot is worth more to the business that holds it.

      What’s the setup fee on Fix & Track?

      It covers the fixes we make in your first month: your Google profile, website pages and listings. Every business starts in a different place, so we quote it after looking at your free check and your online presence.

      What if my area is full?

      You can still start with Track, and we’ll let you know as soon as a Fix & Track or Grow spot opens for your type of business.

      My business sells across a whole province or all of Canada. How does that work?

      Province-wide and national plans are quoted for your business. Because you’d hold one of the three spots in every area you cover, the price reflects the cities and towns included. Email us and we’ll put a quote together. Chains and franchises that want to show up town by town are quoted the same way.

      My business serves several towns. Do I pay for each one?

      No. Your home area is included. You only pay for extra areas if you’re on Fix & Track or Grow and want us to track and work on those towns too.
